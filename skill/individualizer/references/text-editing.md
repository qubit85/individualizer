# Natural prose editing

Use with the relevant language guide. These are editorial heuristics, not a classifier of who wrote a text.

## Select edit depth

Respect an explicit depth; otherwise choose the least intervention that solves the problem.

- **Light:** local phrase cleanup, grammar, and small rhythm repairs; preserve argument and paragraph order.
- **Standard:** revise clumsy sentences and redundant framing; restructure a paragraph when it improves comprehension.
- **Deep:** rebuild from the source's claims and approved voice when the user requests a substantial rewrite. Do not silently turn a point edit into this mode.

For a narrow request, edit only the requested span plus any necessary grammatical connection. Existing natural prose may need no change.

For a revision of an existing draft, use [Learning from revisions](learning-from-revisions.md): carry forward the user's prior corrections, identify the new feedback, and check the result against both. When a profile is selected, apply its active learned rules only in their matching language and context.

## Four passes

1. **Meaning inventory.** Identify the claims, limits, evidence, intended action, and immutable spans. A short piece needs only a mental inventory; use an explicit checklist for dense or consequential text. Distinguish unsupported promotional assertions from supported facts.
2. **Reader and voice.** Put the useful point where this reader expects it. Select vocabulary, emotional temperature, formality, and structure using the brief and approved samples. Keep useful greetings in email, definitions in technical work, and genre-appropriate conclusions.
3. **Editorial revision.** Address paragraph-level templates before word substitutions. Remove filler, fix relations between ideas, replace empty abstractions with supported content, and vary rhythm where the argument benefits. Read for flow; do not simulate spontaneity with errors, random fragments, slang, or punctuation quotas.
4. **Fidelity comparison.** Compare against the source: every material claim must survive unless its removal was requested. Check negation, qualifications, attribution, sequence, quantities, link destinations, and protected spans. Restore anything lost. Remove any invented detail. Re-read the changed passages once for coherence.

If evidence is missing, preserve the limitation and ask only when it blocks the requested result. Do not silently fact-check by changing substantive claims during a style-only edit. Flag questionable claims separately; for marketing approval, leave them unapproved until supported.

## Review patterns in context

| Pattern | Useful intervention | Leave it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad opener that fits almost any topic | Start with the actual situation or proposition | The historical context contains necessary information |
| Repeated rhetorical reveal or false contrast | State the relationship directly | Both alternatives matter or it corrects a real misconception |
| Lists of near-synonyms, automatic triads | Keep the distinct ideas and their real relationships | There are genuinely three requirements |
| Summary after every example | Remove a sentence that only repeats the example | It adds a consequence the reader could miss |
| Inflated praise, unnamed authority | Use supported features; retain attribution and its limits | A quoted opinion or sourced assessment matters |
| Clusters of fashionable abstract words | Choose precise language for this subject | A term is established, exact, or characteristic of the writer |
| Uniform paragraph rhythm and decorative headings | Organize around the argument, not an output template | A repeated structure aids comparison or accessibility |
| Elaborate synonym cycling | Use a stable term for the same concept | The referent or meaning actually changes |
| Chatty wrappers inside a finished artifact | Remove process chatter | A greeting, thanks, or personal aside serves the genre |

Keep epistemic qualifiers. “May” and “might” are not clutter if they encode uncertainty. Do not replace a generic claim with an invented number or anecdote.

## Operational output

Deliver only the final prose when the user asks for it. For a review, show a few representative findings with their reason and corrected passage. Report a retained disputed claim outside the finished prose, unless the brief requires it inside.

No human-likeness percentage, detector pass rate, or “AI-free” certificate. The evidence of improvement is a readable result, preserved meaning, and fidelity to the chosen voice.
