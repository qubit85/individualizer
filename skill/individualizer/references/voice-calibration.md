# Calibrating a reusable voice

Use for style discovery, extracting preferences from examples, or fixing persistent voice drift. A one-off rewrite can use available context without this full process.

## Gather useful evidence

Read existing approved profiles first. Ask what the person creates, for whom, and what effect they want. Optional samples are especially helpful: a passage they wrote, a passage they like, and one they dislike. Do not require all three or ask a long survey.

Record whether a sample is the user's own writing, an admired reference, or an AI draft. Permission to inspect a sample does not imply permission to publish it. Extract general traits; avoid copying distinctive third-party passages.

## Infer concrete traits

Describe observable choices: direct versus gradual openings, sentence complexity, terminology, humor, emotional restraint, paragraph organization, opinion strength, and reader address. Ground each observation in a short pointer to a sample or explicit feedback.

Distinguish stable voice from contextual tone. A person may be warm in support, urgent during an incident, and playful in a personal essay while remaining precise throughout. Do not force a motif into every sentence or a persuasive CTA into every medium.

Mark inferences as provisional. Do not infer age, personality, credentials, experiences, or demographic identity from writing style.

## Calibrate with a controlled comparison

Use the same short fact set for contrasting samples so the user chooses style rather than different information. Offer two or three directions when exploration is needed; fewer suffice for a specific revision. Let the user combine traits.

Show how each direction behaves in a second relevant context. Ask which choices feel natural and what feels wrong. Record concrete feedback rather than an invented satisfaction score.

The approved profile should contain examples that demonstrate the rules, plus exceptions or anti-examples where useful. A recurring syntactic pattern is optional, not a requirement that every artifact repeat a formula.

## Evolve carefully

A request such as “make this email warmer” changes this email. “Use a warmer tone for all future support replies” authorizes a profile change in that context. Update JSON and Markdown together, preserve unrelated preferences, and record the change. Never promote a generated demonstration to approved status without user confirmation.
